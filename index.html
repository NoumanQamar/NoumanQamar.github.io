FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which projects can I compare?

Only projects with the same Project ID can be compared. The feature compares two versions of the same project, such as the current plan against a baseline, saved snapshots, or updates over time.

What do the two sets of bars mean?

The top-oriented bars show the earlier version (for example the baseline) and the bottom-oriented bars show the current update. Where the two bars no longer line up, the activity has moved — and the table tells you by how many days.

Which changes does the comparison detect?

Start and finish date shifts, duration changes, progress (% complete) changes, renamed activities, plus activities that were added to or deleted from the newer version.

Do I need Primavera P6 installed?

No. MegaProjectPlanner runs in the browser. Create a free account, open your workspace, and work with the built-in sample megaproject or edit the update version activity by activity to model your own programme.

Can I share the results of a comparison?

Yes — the Compare view exports a CSV change report with every activity, its status and its variance, ready to attach to a progress report or an extension-of-time submission.
